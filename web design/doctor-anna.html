MUDr. Anna Nováková

Internal medicine & preventive care

Department: General Practice & Internal Medicine · Location: 2nd floor, room 204
Speaks: Czech, English (example data)

Profile

MUDr. Anna Nováková is an internal medicine doctor focusing on long-term care for adults and comprehensive preventive check-ups. She helps patients manage chronic conditions and coordinates follow-up care across different specialties so that treatment plans remain clear and consistent.

Her interests include cardiovascular risk prevention, lifestyle medicine and early detection of common internal diseases through regular screening and check-ups.

Approach to patients

During consultations, Dr. Nováková takes time to listen to patients’ concerns and explain diagnoses in understandable language. She prefers shared decision-making – patients are encouraged to ask questions and together they agree on realistic next steps.

She combines evidence-based medicine with practical advice for everyday life, for example how to adjust diet, movement and sleep to support long-term health.

Clinical focus

  • Long-term management of hypertension, type 2 diabetes and high cholesterol
  • Individualised preventive check-ups and screening programmes for adults
  • Coordination of care with Cardiology, Endocrinology and other specialties
  • Lifestyle counselling focused on movement, nutrition and stress management
