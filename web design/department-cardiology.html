Cardiology

Diagnostics and monitoring of heart and vascular diseases in this fictional clinic.

What we do

The Cardiology department focuses on patients with suspected or confirmed heart disease. The goal is early diagnosis, risk reduction and long-term follow-up.

  • Assessment of chest pain, palpitations and shortness of breath
  • ECG and echocardiography (heart ultrasound) as example examinations
  • Long-term follow-up after heart attack (example scenario)
  • Optimisation of medication and cardiovascular risk factors
