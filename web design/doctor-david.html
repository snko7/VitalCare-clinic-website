MUDr. David Horák

Cardiology & cardiovascular prevention

Department: Cardiology · Location: 3rd floor, room 312
Speaks: Czech, English (example data)

Profile

MUDr. David Horák is a fictional cardiologist focusing on early detection and long-term follow-up of cardiovascular diseases. In this student project, he represents the specialist care available at VitalCare Clinic.

His work centres on reducing the risk of heart attack and stroke through a combination of diagnostics, medication and lifestyle changes.

Approach to patients

During consultations, Dr. Horák explains test results in clear language and uses visual aids where appropriate. Patients are invited to ask questions and to actively participate in decisions about their treatment.

He focuses on small, realistic steps – for example gradual changes in physical activity, diet and stress management.

Clinical focus

  • Diagnostics of chest pain, palpitations and shortness of breath
  • Management of high blood pressure and high cholesterol
  • Follow-up care after heart attack (example scenario)
  • Cardiovascular risk assessment and prevention
