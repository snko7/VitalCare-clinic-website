MUDr. Petra Malá

Neurology & headache clinic

Profile

MUDr. Petra Malá is a fictional neurologist with a focus on headaches, dizziness and long-term neurological conditions. In this student prototype she represents specialist neurological care.

Approach to patients

Dr. Malá explains examinations such as MRI or nerve conduction tests in simple terms and discusses what each result means for everyday life. She emphasises realistic expectations and gradual progress.

Clinical focus

  • Diagnosis and management of migraines and chronic headaches
  • Assessment of balance problems, dizziness and neuropathies
  • Long-term follow-up for chronic neurological diseases (example)
  • Cooperation with Rehabilitation & Physiotherapy for movement difficulties
