Orthopedics

Care for joints, bones and ligaments, including sports injuries and chronic orthopedic problems.

What we do

The Orthopedics department diagnoses and treats movement-related problems. In this fictional clinic, orthopedists cooperate closely with Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation.

  • Consultations for knee, hip, shoulder and spine complaints
  • Treatment of sports injuries and overuse syndromes
  • Pre- and post-operative planning for orthopedic surgery
  • Referral to physiotherapy for safe return to daily activities
