Mgr. Jana Králová

Physiotherapy & rehabilitation

Department: Rehabilitation & Physiotherapy · Location: 1st floor, room 112
Speaks: Czech, English (example data)

Profile

Mgr. Jana Králová is a physiotherapist specialising in functional movement, post-operative rehabilitation and long-term musculoskeletal problems. Her goal is to help patients return to everyday activities with less pain and more confidence.

Approach to patients

She builds individual therapy plans based on a detailed assessment of movement and lifestyle. Jana explains each exercise clearly and checks that patients understand how to continue safely at home. She prefers active therapy and patient education over passive procedures only.

Clinical focus

  • Rehabilitation after surgery or fractures
  • Back and neck pain related to sedentary work or overload
  • Sports injuries and return-to-sport programmes
  • Individual exercise plans for chronic musculoskeletal problems
