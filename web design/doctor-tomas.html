MUDr. Tomáš Svoboda

Orthopedics & sports injuries

Department: Orthopedics · Location: 2nd floor, room 218
Speaks: Czech, English (example data)

Profile

MUDr. Tomáš Svoboda is an orthopedic surgeon with a focus on joint and ligament problems, sports injuries and conservative treatment options. He works with both recreational and professional athletes as well as patients with chronic orthopedic issues.

Approach to patients

Dr. Svoboda explains treatment options transparently, including when surgery is really necessary and when targeted rehabilitation is sufficient. He cooperates closely with physiotherapists to ensure a safe return to normal movement or sport.

Clinical focus

  • Diagnosis and treatment of knee, hip and shoulder problems
  • Sports injuries of ligaments and tendons
  • Consultations before and after orthopedic surgery
  • Long-term care for degenerative joint disease
