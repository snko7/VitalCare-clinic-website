MUDr. Pavel Černý

Pediatrics & family medicine

Department: Pediatrics · Location: 3rd floor, room 305
Speaks: Czech, English (example data)

Profile

MUDr. Pavel Černý is a pediatrician focused on the long-term health and development of children from birth to adolescence. He combines evidence-based medicine with a calm and friendly approach that helps children and parents feel safe and informed.

Approach to patients

During consultations, Dr. Černý takes time to listen to parents' concerns and explain diagnoses in clear language. He emphasises prevention, vaccination and healthy lifestyle, and always encourages parents to ask questions so they leave the visit with confidence.

Clinical focus

  • Preventive check-ups and vaccination schedules for children
  • Management of common childhood illnesses and allergies
  • Monitoring growth and development, including school-age problems
  • Consultations for parents on nutrition, sleep and daily routines
