How to Get Here

Public Transport

Take tram line 10 or 22 to Vinohrady stop. The clinic is a 2-minute walk from the tram station.

Metro

The nearest metro station is Náměstí Míru (Line A). Walk towards Vinohradská Street – approx. 8 minutes.

By Car

Short-term parking is available near the clinic. Please follow local parking signs and rules.
